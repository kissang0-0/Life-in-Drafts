# FEATURE: CLOUD CORNER

## Purpose

Cloud Corner is the emotional intelligence center of Life in Drafts.

This feature analyzes data from every section of the app and transforms it into gentle insights.

Not statistics.

Not spreadsheets.

Stories and patterns.

The goal is self-understanding.

---

# CORE CONCEPT

Cloud Corner answers:

How have I been?

What patterns exist in my life?

How am I growing?

---

# MAIN DASHBOARD

Display floating cloud cards.

Each cloud contains:

* Insight
* Reflection
* Trend
* Pattern

The dashboard should feel dreamy and calm.

---

# LIFE OVERVIEW

Display:

* Current mood trend
* Productivity trend
* Study trend
* Wellness trend

Use clouds instead of charts when possible.

---

# MOOD INSIGHTS

Analyze:

* Mood Garden
* Dear Me
* Daily check-ins

Examples:

"You felt happiest on weekends this month."

"Calm moods increased by 18%."

"Anxious entries appeared less frequently."

---

# ENERGY INSIGHTS

Analyze:

* Energy levels
* Study sessions
* Cycle & Error

Examples:

"Your energy tends to peak around midday."

"High-energy days often align with study success."

---

# STUDY INSIGHTS

Connect with Study Sanctuary.

Examples:

"You studied most consistently on Tuesdays."

"45-minute sessions are your most productive."

"Math received the most focus this month."

---

# TO DO/N'T INSIGHTS

Analyze:

* Completed tasks
* Missed tasks
* Habits

Examples:

"You complete health-related tasks most often."

"Doomscrolling was avoided 12 times this month."

---

# CYCLE & ERROR INSIGHTS

Examples:

"Low energy appears most often before your period."

"You tend to journal more during luteal days."

"Focus improves during follicular phase."

Present gently.

Never present as medical advice.

---

# UNSOCIAL ME-DIA INSIGHTS

Examples:

"You posted most often on Fridays."

"Growth-related posts increased this month."

"Your most common tag is #school."

---

# UNSENT MESSAGE INSIGHTS

Examples:

"Most messages were written to Future Me."

"Gratitude messages increased recently."

"Closure-related messages became less frequent."

---

# MEMORY INSIGHTS

Examples:

"Most saved memories happened during summer."

"You save more achievement memories than photo memories."

---

# LIFE WEATHER SYSTEM

Create a monthly emotional weather report.

Examples:

☀️ Mostly Sunny Month

☁️ Mixed Skies

🌧 Reflective Season

🌈 Growth After Rain

Generated from mood data.

---

# MONTHLY REFLECTION

Automatically generate summaries.

Examples:

"This month you wrote 14 diary entries, completed 42 tasks, studied 23 hours, and saved 8 memories."

Add gentle narrative observations.

---

# YEAR IN DRAFTS

Annual review feature.

Display:

* Mood timeline
* Favorite memories
* Biggest achievements
* Most active months
* Growth highlights

Like Spotify Wrapped, but for life.

---

# GROWTH TIMELINE

Show personal growth milestones.

Examples:

First Diary Entry

100th Task

1 Year Journal Streak

100 Study Hours

---

# CLOUD FORECAST

Generate predictive insights.

Examples:

"You're usually most productive during the first week of the month."

"Journaling tends to improve your mood."

These should be observational, not deterministic.

---

# NIMBUS INSIGHTS

Nimbus explains patterns.

Examples:

"You've been showing up for yourself consistently."

"Progress isn't always visible, but it's here."

"Your garden has grown a lot recently."

---

# VISUAL DESIGN

Use:

* Floating clouds
* Soft animations
* Sky gradients
* Light particles
* Gentle transitions

Clouds should move slowly.

The screen should feel alive.

---

# EMPTY STATE

Display small cloud drifting in sky.

Nimbus flying nearby.

Message:

"The more you live, the more patterns you'll discover."

---

# USER EXPERIENCE GOAL

Cloud Corner should feel like a quiet conversation with yourself.

Not a dashboard.

Not an analytics page.

A place where your memories, moods, habits, studies, and experiences come together to tell the story of your growth.

A bird's-eye view of:

The Archive of Becoming.
