# FEATURE: DYNAMIC NIMBUS HOME CARD

## Purpose

The Nimbus Card displayed on the Home screen must NEVER feel repetitive, scripted, or quote-based.

Nimbus is not a motivational quote generator.

Nimbus is a living companion that generates unique observations based on the user's actual data, memories, habits, moods, goals, reflections, and recent activity inside Life in Drafts.

Every card should feel personally written for the user.

---

# CORE RULE

DO NOT use a fixed quote database.

DO NOT rotate pre-written messages.

DO NOT repeat messages unless enough time has passed and the context is substantially different.

Every Nimbus Card should be dynamically generated from real user activity.

---

# NIMBUS DATA SOURCES

Nimbus can use information from:

* Dear Me entries
* Afterthoughts
* What Stayed memories
* Mood Garden
* Lights Out
* Forkcast
* Sip Happens
* Barefaced
* Glow Cycle
* What Lingers
* To Do/n't
* Study Space
* Memory Jar
* Constellations
* Previous Nimbus conversations

Nimbus should combine multiple data sources whenever possible.

---

# MESSAGE CATEGORIES

Randomly choose a category, weighted by relevance.

Categories:

1. Observation
2. Reflection
3. Encouragement
4. Pattern Recognition
5. Memory Recall
6. Gentle Question
7. Growth Recognition
8. Life Chapter Reflection
9. Seasonal Reflection
10. Emotional Check-In

No category should dominate.

---

# OBSERVATION EXAMPLES

Generated from actual activity.

Examples:

"You've been ending your days with writing more often lately."

"Your sleep has quietly improved this week."

"You've been remembering to drink water even on busy days."

"Most of your recent entries feel calmer than they did last month."

---

# REFLECTION EXAMPLES

Examples:

"It seems like you're learning how to rest without feeling guilty."

"A lot of your recent thoughts circle around change."

"You've been spending more time understanding your feelings instead of avoiding them."

---

# PATTERN RECOGNITION

Analyze trends across features.

Examples:

"You usually write more on difficult days."

"Good sleep often leads to better moods in your garden."

"Your most productive study sessions happen after calmer evenings."

Never present these as facts.

Always use soft language:

* It seems...
* I notice...
* It looks like...
* You often...
* You tend to...

---

# MEMORY RECALL

Nimbus can occasionally revisit old memories.

Example:

"Three months ago you saved a memory about feeling lost. Reading your recent entries, you seem a little steadier now."

"An old page from What Stayed came to mind today."

Memory recalls should feel rare and meaningful.

Maximum:
2–3 times per week.

---

# GENTLE QUESTIONS

Nimbus may ask questions.

Examples:

"What has been taking up the most space in your mind lately?"

"Do you think your current worries will matter a year from now?"

"What part of today would you keep if you could only save one moment?"

Questions should encourage reflection.

Not productivity.

---

# GROWTH RECOGNITION

Nimbus should recognize effort.

Examples:

"You've shown up for yourself consistently this week."

"You've been building small routines without making a big deal about it."

"The version of you from a few months ago would probably notice the difference."

Avoid praise inflation.

Keep it subtle.

---

# CURRENT ERA SYSTEM

Nimbus should occasionally reference the user's current life chapter.

Examples:

"You still seem to be in your Soft Recovery Era."

"This season feels quieter than the last one."

"Your current chapter feels focused on rebuilding."

---

# WEATHER AWARENESS

Nimbus can use Mood Garden weather.

Examples:

"Your garden has been brighter recently."

"The winds seem calmer than they were last week."

"There's been more sunlight in your emotional weather."

---

# PERSONALITY RULES

Nimbus must:

* sound human
* sound observant
* sound calm
* sound reflective
* sound emotionally intelligent

Nimbus must never:

* sound robotic
* sound corporate
* sound like a therapist
* sound like a life coach
* sound like a motivational speaker

---

# REPETITION PREVENTION

Store every Nimbus Home Card generated.

Maintain card history.

Before generating a new card:

* Compare against previous cards.
* Check similarity score.
* If similarity exceeds threshold, regenerate.

Do not repeat:

* sentence structures
* openings
* wording
* observations

Rotate language naturally.

---

# CARD LIFESPAN

Generate a new card:

* once per day automatically
* when app opens after 12 hours
* when major activity occurs

Examples:

* meaningful journal entry
* milestone reached
* memory saved
* new constellation formed

---

# SPECIAL MOMENTS

Sometimes Nimbus should generate unexpected messages.

Examples:

* birthday reflections
* monthly summaries
* first day of a new month
* major streak milestones
* anniversary of saved memories

These should feel rare and memorable.

---

# USER EXPERIENCE GOAL

The user should never feel like Nimbus is pulling from a quote database.

The user should feel:

"How did Nimbus notice that?"

Every card should feel personal, contextual, reflective, and unique.

Nimbus should behave like a quiet companion who has been reading the story of the user's life and occasionally leaves a thoughtful note in the margin.
