FEATURE: BAREFACED
Skincare & Self-Care Ritual Tracker
Purpose

Create a skincare and self-care tracking feature called Barefaced.

Barefaced is not just a checklist. It is a gentle ritual tracker that helps the user care for their skin, notice patterns, and build consistency without pressure.

It should feel like a calming mirror moment, not a beauty standard app.

It connects with:

Lights Out (sleep quality affects skin)
Sip Happens (hydration affects skin glow)
Forkcast (nutrition affects skin health)
Glow Cycle (hormonal changes)
Nimbus (self-care reminders)
Visual Concept

Barefaced is designed like a soft mirror interface.

Frosted glass panels
Gentle reflections
Blue-white lighting
Floating skincare icons (not emojis)

Skin is represented as a living glow meter, not a score.

Skin sensitivity increases

Integration with Sip Happens

Integration with Forkcast
