# FEATURE: STUDY SANCTUARY

## Purpose

Study Sanctuary is the academic hub of Life in Drafts.

It should help the user:

* Study consistently
* Track academic progress
* Build focus habits
* Manage deadlines
* Prepare for exams
* Reflect on learning

The experience should feel calm, motivating, and cozy.

Not stressful.

Not corporate.

Not overly competitive.

The goal is progress, not perfection.

---

# MAIN DASHBOARD

Display:

* Today's study time
* Weekly study time
* Current focus streak
* Upcoming deadlines
* Current subject focus
* Active study goals

Show a greeting from Nimbus.

Examples:

"One page at a time."

"Progress counts, even when it's slow."

---

# SUBJECT MANAGEMENT

Allow creating subjects.

Fields:

* Subject Name
* Subject Color
* Subject Icon
* Teacher (optional)
* Notes (optional)

Examples:

📚 English

➗ Mathematics

🔬 Science

💻 Programming

🎨 Art

Custom subjects allowed.

---

# SUBJECT PAGE

Each subject should have its own page.

Display:

* Total hours studied
* Upcoming assessments
* Notes
* Progress charts
* Recent study sessions

# STUDY SESSION SYSTEM

* Yes
