# FEATURE: CYCLE & ERROR

## Purpose

Cycle & Error is the personal menstrual cycle and wellness companion inside Life in Drafts.

The goal is to help the user understand patterns between:

* Menstrual cycle
* Mood
* Energy
* Productivity
* Study habits
* Emotional well-being

This feature should feel supportive, insightful, and cozy.

Never clinical.

Never overwhelming.

Never judgmental.

The goal is awareness, not diagnosis.

---

# MAIN DASHBOARD

Display:

* Current Cycle Day
* Current Cycle Phase
* Days Until Next Period
* Today's Mood
* Today's Energy
* Recent Symptoms

Include a gentle Nimbus message.

Examples:

"Your body is doing important work."

"Be extra kind to yourself today."

"Rest is productive too."
