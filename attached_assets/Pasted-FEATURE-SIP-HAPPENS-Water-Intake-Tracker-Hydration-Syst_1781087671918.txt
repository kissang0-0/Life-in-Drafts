FEATURE: SIP HAPPENS
Water Intake Tracker (Hydration System)
Purpose

Create a hydration tracking feature called Sip Happens.

This feature helps the user build a consistent water intake habit in a cute, visual, and satisfying way.

It should feel light, rewarding, and integrated into daily life—not like a strict health requirement.

Sip Happens is deeply connected to:

Forkcast (nutrition)
Mood Garden (emotional state)
Glow Cycle (wellness system)
Cloud Corner (insights)
Nimbus (companion encouragement)
Visual Concept

Water is represented as a living cloud system.

Instead of bottles or glasses:

A soft blue cloud floats on screen
It fills with rain droplets as the user drinks water
When hydrated, the cloud becomes bright and fluffy
When dehydrated, it becomes small and sleepy

Everything should feel soft, cute, and animated.

Display:

Allow users to log water in:

Glasses (250ml default)
Bottles (500ml / 750ml / 1L)
Custom ml input

💧 +500ml
💧 +1L

Animation System

Each water log triggers:

Raindrops falling into cloud
Gentle sparkle effect
Flower growth in Mood Garden

If goal reached:

Full rain animation
Rainbow appears briefly
Nimbus celebrates

Track:

Daily hydration streaks
Weekly consistency

21 Days
Integration with Forkcast

Water affects Forkcast dashboard:

Examples:

Better hydration → improved energy insight
Low hydration → Nimbus gentle reminder

6 / 8 glasses

Shared data source.

Mood Garden Connection

Water directly affects Mood Garden:

Well hydrated → flowers bloom brighter
Low hydration → garden looks dry or cloudy

This creates emotional feedback loop.

Nimbus Hydration Companion

Nimbus gives soft reminders:

Examples:

“You’ve only had 2 glasses today. Want to give your cloud a little rain?”

“Your garden might need water too 🌱”

“Small sips still count.”

Avoid pressure or guilt language.
