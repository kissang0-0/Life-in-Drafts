# FEATURE: MOOD GARDEN

## Purpose

Mood Garden is a visual representation of the user's emotional journey.

Instead of charts and numbers, moods become living flowers growing in a personal garden.

Every diary entry, mood check-in, and emotional reflection contributes to the garden.

The goal is to make emotional tracking feel beautiful, calming, and rewarding.

---

# CORE CONCEPT

Every recorded mood creates a flower.

Each flower should be a fully illustrated plant with:

* Stem
* Leaves
* Petals
* Bloom animation

DO NOT use emoji flowers.

DO NOT use flat icons.

Flowers should look like real illustrated flowers growing from the ground.

The garden should feel alive.

# FLOWER GENERATION

Every mood entry plants a flower.

Each flower stores:

* Date
* Mood
* Weather
* Energy level
* Related diary entry

Flower becomes clickable.

Opening flower shows:

* Mood
* Date
* Journal entry
* Notes
* Photos

---

# GARDEN LAYOUT

Display flowers inside a scrollable garden.

Not a grid.

Not a list.

Flowers should appear naturally placed.

The garden should slowly become fuller over time.

---

# FLOWER GROWTH SYSTEM

Flowers grow based on consistency.

Day 1:
Seedling

Day 3:
Small Plant

Day 7:
Young Flower

Day 30:
Fully Bloomed Flower

Long streaks create larger flowers.

---

# SEASONAL CHANGES

Garden changes based on month.

Spring:
Fresh blooms

Summer:
Bright colors

Autumn:
Falling leaves

Winter:
Snow effects

Season changes automatically.

---

# WEATHER INTEGRATION

Use the weather selected in Dear Me.

The entire garden environment changes.

---

## SUNNY DAY

If journal entry weather is Sunny:

Add:

* Sun rays
* Light particles
* Soft sunshine glow
* Butterfly animations

Garden appears brighter.

---

## CLOUDY DAY

If weather is Cloudy:

Add:

* Slow moving clouds
* Softer lighting
* Cool color tones

---

## RAINY DAY

If weather is Rainy:

Add:

* Falling rain
* Water droplets on flowers
* Gentle ripple effects
* Reflection puddles

Flowers sway slightly.

---

## STORMY DAY

If weather is Stormy:

Add:

* Dark clouds
* Wind animations
* Occasional distant lightning
* Strong flower movement

Keep cozy, not scary.

---

## WINDY DAY

If weather is Windy:

Add:

* Grass movement
* Flower swaying
* Floating leaves
* Drifting petals

Entire garden feels alive.

---

## NIGHT

If weather is Night:

Add:

* Moonlight
* Fireflies
* Stars
* Glow effects

Moonflowers bloom brighter.

---

# DYNAMIC ENVIRONMENT

The garden should never feel static.

Continuously animate:

* Grass movement
* Flower swaying
* Clouds
* Floating particles
* Butterflies
* Fireflies

Animations should be subtle.

Never distracting.

---

# GARDEN PATHS

As more entries are created:

Small pathways appear.

Examples:

10 entries:
Stone path appears.

50 entries:
Garden bridge appears.

100 entries:
Pond appears.

250 entries:
Gazebo appears.

500 entries:
Entire sanctuary expands.

The garden visually grows with the user's life.
