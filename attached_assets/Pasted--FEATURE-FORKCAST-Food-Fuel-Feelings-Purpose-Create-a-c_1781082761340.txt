🍓 FEATURE: FORKCAST
Food, Fuel & Feelings
Purpose

Create a complete nutrition, calorie deficit, meal logging, and food reflection feature called Forkcast.

Forkcast should help users build healthier eating habits, maintain a calorie deficit, track nutrition, and understand how food affects their body and mood.

The experience should feel supportive, cute, and encouraging.

Avoid:

Diet culture language
Shame-based messaging
Harsh red warnings
Punishment systems

Focus on:

Consistency
Self-awareness
Sustainable habits

The visual style must match Life in Drafts:

Soft blue theme
Cute blueberry-inspired accents
Rounded cards
Cozy illustrations
Gentle animations

User Experience Goal

Forkcast should feel like a friendly nutrition companion.

Not a diet app.

Not a calorie punishment system.

A place where the user learns how food fuels their body, supports their goals, and becomes part of their journey through The Archive of Becoming.
