# FEATURE: ME, MYSELF & I

## Purpose

Me, Myself & I is the personal profile and identity hub of Life in Drafts.

This is where the user can see:

* Who they are
* Who they have been
* Who they are becoming

This page should feel like a personal scrapbook, life dashboard, and self-portrait combined.

It is the emotional center of the app.

Not a settings screen.

Not a statistics page.

A reflection of the person behind the entries.

---

# PROFILE HEADER

Display:

* Profile photo
* Name
* Nickname
* Personal quote
* Join date

Example:

Life in Drafts Member Since:
June 2026

# USER EXPERIENCE GOAL

A place to remember.

A place to reflect.

A place to dream.

A place to see how much you've grown.

Not who others think you are.

But who you know yourself to be.

The center of:

Life in Drafts

The Archive of Becoming.
