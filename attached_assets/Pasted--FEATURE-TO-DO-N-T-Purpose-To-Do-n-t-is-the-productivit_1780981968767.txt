# FEATURE: TO DO/N'T

## Purpose

To Do/n't is the productivity and accountability system of Life in Drafts.

Unlike a traditional to-do list, it manages both:

### To Do

Things I want to accomplish.

### To Don't

Things I want to avoid.

The goal is to help the user build good habits, avoid bad habits, and document progress.

---

# Main Screen

Divide the screen into two sections.

### TO DO

Positive actions.

Examples:

* Finish assignment
* Study for 1 hour
* Drink water
* Exercise
* Read a book

---

### TO DON'T

Behaviors to avoid.

Examples:

* Doomscrolling
* Procrastinating
* Skipping meals
* Sleeping late
* Excessive spending

---

# User Experience Goal

The user should feel:

Motivated.

Capable.

Accountable.

Proud.

To Do/n't should not feel like a boring checklist.

It should feel like a visual record of effort, growth, discipline, and progress.
